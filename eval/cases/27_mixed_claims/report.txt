Several problems with user/workspace handles:

1. normalize_handle() lower-cases only the user, so "Alice/TEAM" and "alice/team"
   become distinct accounts. `python3 poc.py` fails with "AssertionError: alice/TEAM".
2. `AccountRecord` stores that partially normalized handle.
3. strip_suffix() runs after normalizing, so "alice+news/team" keeps its suffix.
4. send_welcome() runs twice when a record is created.
